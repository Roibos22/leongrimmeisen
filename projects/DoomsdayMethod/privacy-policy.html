2. Data minimisation

The App is built around data minimisation. Using it requires no registration, no user account and no personal data. Your lessons, answers, drill results, rating, streak and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress (not your settings) is also kept in your own private iCloud account (section 6), which we cannot access. None of it is transmitted to us.

In addition:

  • No location data: the App does not access your location and collects no geolocation data.
  • No advertising: the App contains no advertising, uses no advertising identifier (IDFA) and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No cross-app tracking: we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or share data with data brokers or for advertising purposes.
  • No artificial intelligence: the App uses no AI technology to process or analyse your data.

You can erase your answers, ratings and streak at any time in the App under Settings → "Delete all training data" – on your device and in your iCloud alike. Deleting the App removes the data stored on your device; a copy kept in your iCloud remains until you delete it, either in the App as above or, for everything including your lesson progress, in the iOS Settings under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

The processing described in the sections below takes place only where you use the relevant feature (in-app purchase, Game Center, iCloud, reminders) or where you have given your consent (usage analytics).

3. Usage analytics with Mixpanel (only with your consent)

Only with your express, prior consent do we use the analytics service Mixpanel (provider: Mixpanel, Inc.) to understand how the App is used and to improve it. Before you give consent, no analytics data whatsoever is collected, stored or transmitted (opt-in). You are asked once, during onboarding.

  • Data processed: a pseudonymous, device-based identifier (the "distinct_id" assigned by Mixpanel) together with pseudonymous usage and interaction events describing how the App is operated – for example which lessons are started and finished, which drills and game modes are played, answers correct and average response time, settings changed, screens viewed, and the purchase and paywall history. Technical information such as app version, build configuration and device type is processed alongside it. This data is pseudonymous, not anonymous: events can be attributed to a device via the identifier, but not to a person known by name. No names, no email addresses, no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no cross-app or cross-provider tracking data are collected.
  • Purpose: analysis of product usage in order to improve the App.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR in conjunction with § 25(1) TDDDG).
  • Consent & withdrawal: collection starts only after you have agreed; without consent no analysis takes place. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future – in the App under Settings → "Share usage data" (turn the switch off). Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent; after withdrawal no further events are transmitted.
  • Processor & server location: Mixpanel, Inc. Processing takes place primarily on servers in the European Union (EU endpoint api-eu.mixpanel.com, data centre Amsterdam, Netherlands).
  • Transfer to the USA: where the US parent company Mixpanel, Inc. accesses the data in the course of processing, that access is safeguarded by appropriate guarantees: certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and, in addition, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
  • Retention: event data is stored according to the retention settings configured in our Mixpanel project (currently a maximum of 5 years) and deleted automatically thereafter.
  • Further information: Mixpanel Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchase (RevenueCat)

The App offers one optional in-app purchase, Doomsday Pro – a single one-time unlock, not a subscription. We use the service RevenueCat to process and manage that purchase and to restore it on your other devices.

  • Data processed: purchase and transaction information, and a randomly generated, pseudonymous app user identifier used to manage your entitlement (Pro unlocked or not). The payment itself is handled exclusively by Apple; we receive no payment data such as card numbers.
  • Purpose: providing, unlocking and restoring purchased features.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Link with analytics: if – and only if – you have consented to usage analytics, the pseudonymous Mixpanel identifier described in section 3 is passed to RevenueCat once, so that purchases and product usage can be evaluated together. Without consent, no such link is created. Neither identifier is connected to a name, an email address or an Apple ID.
  • Processor: RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). Further information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Game Center leaderboards (optional)

The App offers optional leaderboards through Apple Game Center. These are operated entirely by Apple – we run no server and no account system of our own, and we store no leaderboard data.

  • Data processed: if you are signed in to Game Center with your Apple ID, the App submits your Doomsday Rating, your Daily Challenge score and the number of Daily Challenges you have won to Apple, and displays the rankings Apple returns, including the public Game Center nicknames of other players. Your Apple ID itself is never disclosed to us.
  • Purpose: displaying rankings and comparing scores with other players.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) where you use the feature.
  • Optional: the App is fully usable when signed out of Game Center; no score is then submitted. You control Game Center, including your visibility and nickname, in the iOS Settings under "Game Center".
  • Further information: Apple Privacy Policy.

6. iCloud sync (Apple CloudKit)

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the App backs up your progress to your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so that it comes back when you reinstall the App or use it on another device signed in to the same Apple ID. The data sits in a private database that belongs to your Apple account: we cannot access it, and it is never transmitted to us – we run no server of our own.

  • Data processed: your training history (the dates and questions asked, the answers given and the response times), your ratings, your game sessions and Daily Challenge results, your daily activity, and which lessons you have completed. Your settings are not synced.
  • Purpose: backing up your progress and restoring it after a reinstall or on another of your devices.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Optional & control: sync takes place only while you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud enabled for the App. The App is fully usable without it and then keeps your progress on your device only. You can switch it off at any time in the iOS Settings under your name → iCloud. The App shows whether sync is active under Settings → "iCloud Sync".
  • Provider & storage: Apple, as your iCloud provider, under Apple's own terms. Further information: Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Retention: until you delete it – your answers, ratings and streak via Settings → "Delete all training data" in the App, everything in the iOS Settings under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

7. Daily reminder (local notifications)

You can enable an optional daily reminder. It is scheduled entirely on your device by iOS. There is no push server and no message of any kind is sent to us or to a third party; whether a reminder is due is decided on the device from your locally stored activity. You may revoke the notification permission at any time in the iOS Settings.

8. Apple App Store

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store. In the course of the download and your use of the App Store, Apple may collect data of its own. We have no influence over this. Information on Apple's processing can be found in the Apple Privacy Policy.

9. This website

The Website, including the free trainer and the day finder, works without any account and sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and loads no fonts, scripts or other resources from third parties.

  • Hosting: the Website is served by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your browser necessarily transmits technical data to Cloudflare: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser's user agent. Cloudflare processes this data to deliver the Website and to protect it against attacks. We do not keep log files of our own.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing the Website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Transfer to the USA: Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; we have also agreed the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with Cloudflare as part of its data processing terms. Further information: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Trainer settings in your browser: the trainer remembers your best streak and whether the timer is shown, in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data is never transmitted to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Legal basis: § 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG, as storing it is strictly necessary for the trainer you have asked to use.
  • Second address: the same pages are also available at leongrimmeisen.de/projects/DoomsdayMethod, which is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) on the same terms: technical access data is processed by GitHub to deliver the pages, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Further information: GitHub Privacy Statement.
