    Learn it properly

    The app's course walks you from your first weekday to any date, with drills for each step and a rating that tracks your speed.

    How to use the trainer

    Each level trains one part of the Doomsday method, in the order you need it.

    1. Month dates. The twelve dates that always share the year's doomsday. Get these instant first; everything rests on them.
    2. This year. Dates in the current year, with this year's doomsday given. This is steps 3 and 4, which is a real party trick on its own.
    3. Year doomsday. A year from 1900 to 2099: find its doomsday from the century anchor and the year offset. This is steps 1 and 2.
    4. Any date. All four steps together, 1900–2099. That covers almost every date anyone will ask you.
    5. Expert. 1600–2399, so you need all four century anchors.

    Your best streak per level is kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
