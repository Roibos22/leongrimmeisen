Any date. Any year.
In your head.

Doomsday teaches the algorithm that turns a date into a weekday in a few seconds of mental arithmetic. No calendar, no phone, no trick — four small steps, learnable in a couple of weeks.

25 December 2031 — which weekday? 1. The 2000s anchor is Tuesday. 2. Year 31 shifts it three days on: Friday. 3. So the 12th of December is a Friday. 4. The 25th is thirteen days later, which is six past a whole week. Thursday.
