My name is Leon Grimmeisen, and I am a Software Engineer.

When I realized that I could build any app I wanted and load it onto my phone, my passion for software engineering was ignited.

Since that moment, I have been passionately learning about software development and working on projects that solve real problems. As a lifelong learner, I love to use code as my favorite tool and AI as an exciting amplifier to create solutions that make people's lives better.

After sharpening my software engineering skills at 42 Berlin, the school with the best learning concept in the world, I have now embarked on my professional journey as a Backend Engineer — and I ship iOS apps under Roibos Labs on the side.
